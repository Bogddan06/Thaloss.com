About Thaloss

A pond manager, eco-friendly and accessible.

Fish farms and municipal ponds are treated the same way they were forty years ago: lime, chlorine, and someone checking the water by hand, often only after it's already gone wrong. Thaloss replaces that with a small autonomous vessel that lives on the water and treats it continuously through bioremediation, with bacteria and microalgae that consume excess nutrients and organic pollutants as the catamaran glides across the surface.

It runs autonomously for four hours a day, recharging from its own solar panels, while onboard sensors stream water-quality data to the cloud so an operator can see oxygen, pH, and turbidity from their phone instead of a boat.
